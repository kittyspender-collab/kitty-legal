Kitty — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Kitty ("the app", "we") is a personal and family expense tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. In plain terms: your financial data stays on your device. We do not run ads, we do not track you, and we do not sell any data.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter — your kitties (categories), spends, amounts, remarks, budgets, income details, and app settings such as your chosen currency — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData). This information is not transmitted to us or to any server, and we have no access to it.

2. Face ID / biometric lock

If you enable the app lock, Kitty asks iOS to verify your Face ID or device passcode before opening. This check is performed entirely by iOS. Kitty never sees, stores, or transmits your biometric data.

3. Feature requests (optional)

If you choose to submit a feature request from within the app, the text of your request and, if you provide it, the optional name you type are sent to and stored in our backend (Supabase, a third-party hosting provider) so that requests can be listed in the app and reviewed. Do not include sensitive personal information in a feature request. This is the only information the app sends off your device, and only when you actively submit a request.

4. Feedback email (optional)

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in that message (such as your email address and message contents). We use it only to respond to you.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or usage-tracking SDKs.
  • No account or login is required to use the app.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties for their own use.

6. Data retention and deletion

On-device data is removed when you delete the app from your iPhone. For feature requests you submitted, you can ask us to delete them by emailing the address below.

7. Children's privacy

Kitty is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at kittyspender@gmail.com.

Kitty is an independent app. Apple is not a sponsor and is not involved in this policy.
