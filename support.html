Kitty — Support

Kitty helps you and your family track spending, organised into simple "kitties" (categories), with budgets, analytics, an income planner, and optional automatic logging from bank message nudges.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored? Is it backed up?

All your data is stored privately on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer). If you delete the app, its data is removed from the device.

How do I set up automatic spend logging?

Open the app's automation guide and follow the quick setup. You create an iOS Shortcuts automation triggered by your bank's message, which then runs Kitty's "Add a spend" action. Set up separate nudges for your debit and credit-card messages.

How do I change the currency?

Go to Settings → Currency and pick your currency. This updates amounts and the budget symbol across the whole app.

Can I use Kitty with my family?

Kitty is designed around shared "kitties" so a household can organise spending together on a device. Each iPhone keeps its own on-device data.

How do I request a feature?

In the app, open Settings → feedback and use "Request a feature". You can see other requests and add your own. Please don't include sensitive personal information.

How do I delete a feature request I submitted?

Email us at the address above and we'll remove it.
